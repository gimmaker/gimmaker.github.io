How it works

Discord builds link previews by fetching the page and reading its preview tags. X and Instagram give Discord a weak preview without playable video. gimmaker fetches the post itself and hands Discord a preview with the text, photos and the actual video file. Anyone who clicks the link is sent straight to the original post.

You can also fix links by hand: swap x.com or instagram.com for this site's link-fixing address and keep the rest of the link.

Works with public posts only. Source on GitHub.
